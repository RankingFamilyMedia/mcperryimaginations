AutoYaad Ghana — account deletion request

To request deletion of your AutoYaad account and eligible personal information, email mcperryimaginations@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account.

Include in your request

  • Subject: AutoYaad account deletion request
  • Your registered email address and phone number
  • A request to delete your AutoYaad account

What is deleted

We delete or anonymize eligible account profile information, saved vehicles, pending listings and other personal information associated with the account, subject to verification.

What may be retained

Transaction, accounting, security and fraud-prevention records may be retained where required by law, for legitimate dispute handling, or to protect the service. Public vehicle listings may remain visible if they are associated with a completed transaction, but personal contact details will be handled according to the Privacy Policy.
