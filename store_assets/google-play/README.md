# Google Play screenshot assets

These phone screenshots were captured from the connected Samsung device after installing the updated AutoYaad build. They are 720 x 1600 px PNGs and show the real app experience, including the branded splash, marketplace, account, sign-in and registration screens.

Google Play accepts JPEG or 24-bit PNG screenshots with no alpha, a minimum dimension of 320 px and a maximum dimension of 3840 px. For broader recommendation surfaces, Google recommends four portrait screenshots at least 1080 x 1920 px; capture a higher-resolution device set before the final public listing if that eligibility is required.

Replace these demo-preview assets only after the final CMS data, business contact details and production ad configuration are ready.
