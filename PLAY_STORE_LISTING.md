# Play Store listing draft

## Title
AutoYaad Ghana — Cars & Bikes

## Short description
Find managed car, motorbike and commercial-vehicle listings across Ghana.

## Full description
AutoYaad Ghana connects serious buyers with vehicle owners and dealers through a marketplace designed around administrative review and managed transactions.

Browse cars, motorbikes and commercial vehicles; search by make, model or location; view specifications and photographs; submit offers; request assistance; and report suspicious listings. Sellers can submit vehicles, manage listings and follow approval status. AutoYaad administrators review listings and coordinate managed sales.

Verification badges represent separate administrative checks and do not replace an independent mechanical inspection.
